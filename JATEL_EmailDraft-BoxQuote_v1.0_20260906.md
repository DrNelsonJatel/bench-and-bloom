glass bottle, 2.375 in diameter by 5.437 in tall, filled weight 540 g. The

minimum order quantity, any die or setup charge quoted separately, lead time,

## Optional personalisation, one line each

Drop this in after the first paragraph. It is worth doing: it shows you picked them rather than
mass-mailed, and it points each supplier at the box they are likeliest to win.

- **Racer Boxes:** *"I came to you because Box A is a folding carton and you run a dedicated
  folding-carton facility, so you may be able to quote both boxes together."*
- **BC Box Manufacturing:** *"I came to you because Box B has to carry about 1.7 kg of glass, and
  corrugated is likely the right answer for it."*
- **OK Eco-Packaging:** *"I came to you because you are local to us. If collection in West Kelowna
  is an option, that may make up any difference in unit price."*

## Notes

- **Short on purpose.** Suppliers triage a lot of these. Everything above is answerable without
  opening the attachment, which is what gets a reply.
- **Says "pilot" and "small" up front.** Better they decline early than quote and withdraw.
- **Asks for the stock alternative explicitly**, so nobody assumes we want a custom die.
- **No prices, no deadline pressure, no mention of the other two quotes.**
