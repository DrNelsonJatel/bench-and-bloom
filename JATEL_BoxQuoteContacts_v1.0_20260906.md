## Notes

- **Ask all three.** Two Lower Mainland manufacturers plus one local supplier is the right spread:
  the manufacturers will beat the local on unit price, the local may beat both delivered.
- **Say the quantities are a pilot.** All three will have minimums above your 48 bottles. The RFQ
  asks for 100/250/500 on Box A and 50/100/250 on Box B for exactly this reason.
- **Offer the sample bottle.** The supplier version closes with that offer. A converter quoting a
  glass-bottle box would usually rather hold one, and it removes any doubt about the dimensions.
- **Great Little Box (glbc.com)** is the other obvious BC name and has a Kelowna branch, but their
  site blocks automated access so I could not verify an email. Worth a phone call if the three above
  come back thin.
- No prices, lead times or capabilities beyond what each company states publicly have been verified.
  Treat the "best fit" notes as my read of their own marketing, not as a quoted capability.
