## 8. Notes for our own file (not for the supplier)

- Dimensions derive from the S-23397 spec and are QAQC-checked: single clears the bottle by 0.125 in
  diametral and 0.188 in vertical; the row holds three across with 0.125 in slack; the triangle
  geometry is three mutually touching 2.375 in circles.
- **Correction:** earlier notes in this project described the triangle as the *more compact*
  arrangement. It is not. Row footprint is 18.1 sq in against the triangle's 22.2, because a
  triangle wastes the box corners. The triangle's advantage is that it needs **no divider**, not
  that it is smaller.
- Boxes stay unprinted, but an FBA-bound unit still receives hand-applied FNSKU, expiry and brand
  labels. See `JATEL_BoxPackagingSpec_v1.0_20260906.md`.
- A three-pack sold as its own Amazon ASIN needs **its own GTIN**. Unresolved, and it does not
  affect this quote.
