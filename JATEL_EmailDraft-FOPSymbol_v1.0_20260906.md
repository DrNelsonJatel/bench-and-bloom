## What v1.0 said that this does not, and why

| Cut | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Sugars 26 g per 30 mL = 26% DV" | **False precision.** Recipe-derived, never measured, and 0.19 g from a rounding boundary. At the stated 64 °Brix it rounds to **25 g**. Do not commit to a number a refractometer may contradict. Not needed for an artwork request. |
| The Division 18 sweetening-agent exemption paragraph | **Unverified citation.** I have not read the provision and cannot confirm the exemption sits in Division 18. Unnecessary: you are requesting artwork, not an exemption ruling. |
| "Confirm U.15 rather than U.14" | Belongs in the **CFIA Industry Labelling Tool** session. In an artwork request it invites a reference-amount discussion that could delay the files. |
| "This is our first production run" | True, but does nothing for the request. |

**Kept:** the display-surface question. Two sentences, and it saves a round trip on the one sizing
figure that sits near a band boundary.

**Deliberately absent:** pH, Brix and batch data. None bears on the symbol, and the Brix is unmeasured.
